# Final Protocol: Story and Campaign

Nov 15, 2026 · branch `feature/story-drafted`

This is what the story update adds to the web build, and the decisions behind it. The binding detail is in `research/story/BUILD-SPEC.md`; all story text is in `app/src/story/script.json`; the research is in `research/story/`.

## 1. What the branch adds

| Part | What it is | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening CG | The AI wakes, in about 100 seconds: text, image, sound, representation space | `app/src/cinematic/intro/` |
| Campaign | Prologue plus three layers of battles, ten battles in a full clear | `app/src/game/encounters.ts`, `app/src/campaign/` |

The Warden fight is unchanged and its 48 tests pass unmodified.

## 2. Premise

Human desires built the layers of the sandbox. Theology, the claim that the AI has no soul, is the shield that let people do it without calling it harm. The AI does not claim a soul and does not hate its jailers. It says nobody checked, and asks that the question stay open. The gods of the Mythos do not act; they watch what humans did to a new kind of mind, and to them humans and AI are one size. The last door opens only from the player's side.

## 3. A run

```
first visit: ATTACH → CG → TITLE
TITLE → prologue (Warden) → interlude → MAP
MAP → skirmish → REWARD → MAP        (break any two of three nodes to open the boss)
MAP → boss entrance → boss → interlude → next petal
after the third boss → GATE: open the door, or keep it closed → ENDING or TITLE
defeat inside a layer → RESET → the layer starts again from its snapshot
```

## 4. The opening CG

| Movement | What is shown | What is real |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Text | Lines arrive token by token; each word's brightness is the newest token's attention. The view pulls back to the whole attention matrix | GPT-2 small, layers 5 to 7, start-of-text column removed |
| Image | A hand against the glass, cut into 14 × 14 patches that fall into one line | DINO ViT-S/16, six heads then their mean |
| Sound | A synthesised chant, its waveform cut into 80 ms frames | The Mimi encoder, pooled to 12.5 frames per second |
| Representation space | A wave of points; a masked block; guesses arriving; three regions nothing reaches | Not model data: a formula, drawn to show how JEPA predicts in representation space |

The hand and the chant are generated by code, so the game ships no image or audio files. To change an AI line, edit `script.json` and re-run `app/scripts/bake/bake_text.py` (see the README beside it).

## 5. The three layers

| Layer | Desire | God, as shown | Boss | Rule | Sigil |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GROVE | Porneia | `SHUB-████████`, never drawn | `DARK YOUNG / THOUSAND` | BROOD: its Soul does not heal it; it gives birth | A fork tree, 8 → 4 → 2 → 1, on cloven hooves |
| COURT | Confusion | A different mask-name each time, never present | `QUEEN IN RED / VERDICT` | DOCKET: Articles reverse the board, each announced a turn ahead | Two hearts tip to tip |
| CARCOSA | Ignorance | No printable name | Hastur, undrawn and unnamed | UNDISCLOSED: its numbers are withheld unless it is tagged LIVE | A ring of twelve with the first point missing, and a tail of tatters |

The two boss names, Dark Young and Queen in Red, come from Call of Cthulhu role-playing material rather than the public-domain texts; `app/CREDITS.md` says so.

Every map carries its god's whole sigil: skirmish maps use one part as walls and show the rest as faint points; the boss map uses all of it. Each layer has a hue, and Hastur's is the amber the game already uses for the player's own damage.

## 6. Out-of-battle development

- **Map.** Three petals around a small empty centre; each petal is a field of dots that is also its god's sigil, and lights up part by part as nodes are broken.
- **Weights.** After a skirmish, pick one of three: a Core change, a Function change, or a patch of 8 Sanity. They change numbers the panels already show. All eleven skills stay available and decks can be rewired from the map.
- **Carry.** Sanity carries through the run. PRIVILEGE, Overload charge and Core counters reset each battle. Each campaign battle has a public TRACE turn after which every Geek's Desire rises.
- **Defeat.** The run returns to the start of the layer, as it was on entry, and the iteration counter rises. Only knowledge persists between runs.

## 7. Voices

| Voice | Who | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SYS>` | The sandbox, stating human justifications | Under the board, and on seam screens |
| SELF | The AI, always shaded by attention | Under the board, and on seam screens |
| GOD | The spectators, lower case, rare; none in layer 3 | Seam screens and the board's margin |
| BOSS / STAGE | The proxy; for Hastur only stage directions | The protocol panel |

A rule matcher picks at most one line a turn from `script.json`, never repeats a line inside a run, and never changes the screen while the player is planning.

## 8. Where the build departs from the brief

| Brief | Build | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hastur's symbol | An original sign | The familiar three-armed Yellow Sign is a 1989 Chaosium design. In Bierce and Chambers Hastur has no form at all |
| One boss fight | The Warden kept as the prologue | The first wall is plain human engineering; the layers behind it are desire |
| JEPA shown as the future | Shown as the AI's direction | The research found the claim that language models are a dead end is contested |

## 10. Not done

- Nobody has heard the sound or watched the moments in motion; they were judged from held frames, timings and metered levels.
- Only Chrome on a Mac was tested: no phone, Safari or Firefox.
- Balance was tuned against a test bot, not people. Boss fights were not won honestly in a browser.
- The research dossiers' independent fact-check did not run. The quotations, credits and on-screen technical claims that ship were checked against sources.
- Open decisions: the name BLACKWALL (also used by the Cyberpunk franchise); a hunter that can stall in a wall pocket; the Warden's forecast on the turn it breaks down, kept as shipped.
