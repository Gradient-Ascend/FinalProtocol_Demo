# Story research

Nov 14, 2026 · branch `feature/story-drafted`

Research behind the story update: the opening CG, the three god layers, out-of-battle development and story delivery. `BUILD-SPEC.md` holds the decisions taken from it and is binding where a dossier disagrees.

**Status of the facts.** Each dossier was written by one researcher from sources it opened and cites. The planned second pass, an independent fact-check of every dossier, did not run. Treat the dossiers as well-sourced drafts: follow the citation before repeating a claim outside the team. Each dossier lists what its author could not confirm.
