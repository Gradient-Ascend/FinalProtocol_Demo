## 1. Game VFX Design Research Validation & Root-Cause Audit

### 1.1 Validation Against Industry Game VFX Principles

The three-part effect grammar—**Main Effect (traveling to Enemy) + Hit Effect (impact burst at Enemy) + Optional Background Effect (subordinate spatial framing)**—directly aligns with foundational real-time VFX standards across competitive and turn-based games:

1. **Riot Games — *League of Legends VFX Style Guide* (Primary / Secondary / Tertiary Hierarchy & Value Bands):**
   * Prioritizes **Visual Clarity > Minimal Clutter > Thematic Reinforcement > Surprise/Delight**.
   * Separates every spell into **Primary** (unmistakable directional projectile/impact silhouette at `75–95%` contrast), **Secondary** (thematic flame rims/follow-through sparks at `35–65%` value with rapid decay), and **Tertiary / Background** (low-contrast ambient mist or grid framing locked below `22%` alpha so it never obscures board units, routes, or primary silhouettes).
2. **Jason Keyser (*VFX Apprentice*) & GDC *Visual Effects Bootcamp: Artistic Principles of VFX*:**
   * Structures combat abilities into **Launch / Carrier (Main)** -> **Frame-0 Hit Punch + 2–8 Frame Directional Burst (Hit)** -> **Ambient / Zone Layer (Background)**, paired with **The Squint Test**: squinting at any frame must reveal one crisp trajectory and impact focal point rather than an undifferentiated glowing mass.
3. **Turn-Based RPG Layering, AAA Flame Domain Warping & Apple Liquid Glass Techniques:**
   * Stacking unmasked `AdditiveBlending` sprites clips overlapping pixels to flat `(255, 255, 255)` white-out ("light pollution").
   * CoT backgrounds combine **Inigo Quilez 2-stage rotated FBM domain warping**, **Simon Trümpler directional flow advection + Voronoi-FBM alpha erosion**, and **Apple Liquid Glass `smin` union + cohesive liquid meniscus (`~0.14` cell), Beer-Lambert translucent gel body (`alpha <= 0.48`), caustic veins, wet Blinn-Phong speculars (`pow 34`), and `0.32`-cell refractive grid lensing** inside a single `CloudMode.PlasmaFlow` pass (`Z = 0.04`), eliminating straight tracks, bright border rails, foggy mist blur, and overlapping Cross intersection seams while letting random dense eddies and shallow translucent fluid pockets distribute naturally across the grid.

### 1.2 Root-Cause Diagnosis of Prior Build (`ae80352`)

Inspecting `ae80352` identified four concrete causes of light pollution, compactness, and dot clouds:

1. **Low Bloom Threshold & Excessive HDR Multipliers (`vfx-webgl.ts`):** LUT peak gain (`4.5`) paired with a low bloom threshold (`0.76`) and high bloom composite weight (`0.78`) blew overlapping `AdditiveBlending` solids, halos, and decals into white glare on `1×1` cells.
2. **Oversized Compact Solids on `1×1` Target Cells:** `BASE`'s 4 quantized wedges + ghosts + anvil monoliths, `MANHATTAN`'s tall stacked columns, `ARRAY`'s 4 floating corner cages, and `PROBE`'s `sz = 4.5` solid hex prism crammed dense geometry onto single tiles.
3. **Mist Used as Main/Hit Blobs & Mote Dot Clouds in `CoT` (`OFF`, `ON`, `HIGH`, `ULTRA`) + `FLOOD`:** Spawning `25–52` `ParticleShape.Mote` spheres per impact and dense `CloudMode.Mist` puffs turned CoT and `FLOOD` attacks into bright circular dot clouds rather than flowing plasma or liquid crests.
4. **Shared `AttackFx.impact` / `motif` Vestiges in `OVERLOAD` & `ZERO`:** `ZERO`'s `0 ms` instant ray lacked travel readability across the board (`MANIFOLD` solves both naming and spatial wave propagation).
